Decentralized Learning for Coordination in Energy-Constrained Autonomous Agent Swarms
In progress

Research on multi-agent reinforcement learning for coordinating swarms of autonomous agents operating under energy constraints, where agents communicate only when the expected benefit from communication justifies the energy cost. Work is ongoing, with a preprint planned once results are finalized.

Edge AI Across the Compute Spectrum

A single effort to deploy and measure the same machine-learning workload across the full hardware compute spectrum. The project characterizes inference across three distinct compute tiers: a Microcontroller (Tier 1), an Embedded GPU (Tier 2), and a General-Purpose PC (Tier 3) to evaluate the measurable costs of models on radically different hardware.
