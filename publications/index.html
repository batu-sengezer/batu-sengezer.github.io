Research & Publications

Motivation

I like to work on machine learning systems deployed on real, resource-constrained hardware, where every design choice has a measurable cost in memory, latency, and power. My academic goal is to pursue graduate research (MASc or PhD) at the intersection of embedded systems and machine learning, building on a strong foundation in real-time, safety-critical software engineering.

Research Interests

Real-Time Embedded ML

Designing and deploying machine learning models on resource-constrained embedded platforms with an emphasis on deterministic, bounded, and predictable inference latency rather than average-case performance alone.

Edge AI & Acceleration

Understanding when and how specialized hardware acceleration improves on-device AI, accounting for the full system cost of computation, memory movement, communication, power, and accelerator overhead.

Hardware–Software Co-Design

Jointly optimizing algorithms, models, and computing platforms—from microcontrollers and embedded processors to FPGAs and accelerators—to achieve efficient and predictable execution under hardware constraints.

Cyber-Physical & Autonomous Systems

Developing intelligent computing systems that interact with the physical world while operating under constraints in energy, communication, computation, and environmental uncertainty.

Real-Time Safety-Critical Systems

Designing and analyzing computing systems in which timeliness and correctness are both fundamental requirements, and where missed deadlines or incorrect decisions can have physical consequences.

High-Performance & Parallel Computing

Exploring parallel architectures, computation, and scalability as foundations for accelerating machine learning and enabling real-time performance across edge and high-performance computing platforms.
