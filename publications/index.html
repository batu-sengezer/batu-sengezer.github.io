Decentralized Learning for Coordination in Energy-Constrained Autonomous Agent Swarms
In progress

Research on multi-agent reinforcement learning for coordinating swarms of autonomous agents operating under energy constraints, where communication between agents is triggered only when it is worth the cost. Work is ongoing, with a preprint planned once results are finalized.
