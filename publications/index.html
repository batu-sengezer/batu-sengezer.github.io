Research & Publications

I like to work on machine learning systems deployed on real, resource-constrained hardware, where every design choice has a measurable cost in memory, latency, and power. My academic goal is to pursue graduate research (MASc or PhD) at the intersection of embedded systems and machine learning, building on a strong foundation in real-time, safety-critical software engineering.

Real-Time Embedded ML

Deploying neural networks on microcontrollers with provable, cycle-accurate timing guarantees.

Edge AI & Acceleration

Running inference on embedded GPUs and accelerators under real power and thermal limits.

Hardware–Software Co-Design

Mapping computation onto the right hardware — from MCUs to FPGAs — for efficiency and determinism.

Cyber-Physical & Autonomous Systems

Decentralized learning and coordination for constrained autonomous agents.

Decentralized Learning for Coordination in Energy-Constrained Autonomous Agent Swarms
In progress

Research on multi-agent reinforcement learning for coordinating swarms of autonomous agents operating under energy constraints, where agents communicate only when the expected benefit from communication justifies the energy cost. Work is ongoing, with a preprint planned once results are finalized.
